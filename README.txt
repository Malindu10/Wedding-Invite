SHENAYA & SHENAL - ENGAGEMENT INVITATION WEBSITE
================================================

PUT IT ONLINE FOR FREE (Netlify, about 5 minutes)
1. Unzip this file. You get a folder called "shenaya-and-shenal".
2. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop and sign up for a free account (Google sign-in is fine).
3. Drag the whole "shenaya-and-shenal" folder onto that page. Netlify uploads it and gives you a link.
4. In Netlify open your new site, then: Site configuration > Change site name > type  shenaya-and-shenal
   Your link becomes:  https://shenaya-and-shenal.netlify.app
5. Send that link to your guests.

LINK PREVIEW (the picture that shows when the link is shared on WhatsApp)
It is already set up for https://shenaya-and-shenal.netlify.app
If that name is taken and you choose another, open index.html in a text editor and replace
"shenaya-and-shenal.netlify.app" (it appears twice near the top) with your new address,
then upload the folder again (Netlify > your site > Deploys > drag the folder in).

MAKING CHANGES LATER
Ask Claude for the change, unzip the new version, and drag the folder onto
Netlify > your site > Deploys. The link stays the same.
